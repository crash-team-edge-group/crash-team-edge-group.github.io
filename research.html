CRASH (Cryptographic Research and Secure Hardware) is the team within the EDGE Group at the VLSI Lab, Dipartimento di Elettronica e Telecomunicazioni (DET), Politecnico di Torino, working on hardware for post-quantum and lightweight cryptography on RISC-V.

The team builds tightly- and loosely-coupled accelerators for lattice-, code- and hash-based post-quantum schemes (CRYSTALS-Kyber, CRYSTALS-Dilithium, Ascon, CROSS, HQC, LESS), integrates them into RISC-V cores through the Core-V eXtension Interface (CV-X-IF), and evaluates the resulting designs on FPGA and silicon — including their resistance to power side-channel attacks. Work spans the full stack: instruction-set extensions, coprocessor RTL, verification flows, and the true-random-number and hashing primitives the rest of the cryptography depends on.

HORCRUX RISC-V ISE · PQC

A unified, lightweight ISA extension covering every NIST-approved PQC algorithm family.

HORCRUX provides a single Instruction-Set Extension shared across ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA and HQC, instead of one accelerator per scheme. A tightly coupled coprocessor targets the common bottlenecks — modular arithmetic, matrix multiplication and hash transforms — using minimal hardware resources.

On a Zynq UltraScale+ FPGA it reaches up to 3× speedups for lattice- and hash-based schemes and over 30× for code-based schemes, adding under 2,900 LUTs and 400 FFs while staying backward-compatible with standard RISC-V cores.

CIRCE RISC-V ISE · Signatures

A compact RISC-V extension for the CROSS code-based signature scheme.

CIRCE integrates CROSS — built on zero-knowledge proofs and the Restricted Syndrome Decoding Problem — through CV-X-IF, supporting both R-SDP and R-SDP(G) across every official parameter set without hardware retuning.

An average 2× speed-up on a Zynq UltraScale+ FPGA, with a footprint as small as 800 LUTs / 100 FFs.

CHIMERA ASIP · Ascon

An application-specific coprocessor for the Ascon lightweight cryptography standard.

CHIMERA interfaces with a RISC-V core via CV-X-IF and supports Ascon's AEAD (Ascon-128, Ascon-128a) and hashing (Hash, Hasha) modes. Two variants trade off performance and area: a Complete-Round (CR) tightly coupled accelerator, and a Bitwise Rotation Unit (BRU) instruction-set extension.

Implemented on both Zynq UltraScale+ FPGA and ASIC.

ATHOS Hybrid accelerator · X-HEEP

Tightly- and loosely-coupled accelerators for CRYSTALS on the X-HEEP microcontroller.

ATHOS was one of the first real-world uses of the CV-X-IF interface on the CV32E40X core, adding new instructions and external IPs for NTT, INTT and Keccak into the X-HEEP RISC-V microcontroller without touching the toolchain.

On ASIC 65 nm the full architecture costs a 1.47× area overhead while improving total clock cycles for CRYSTALS-Kyber and CRYSTALS-Dilithium by up to 7.74× and 4.12× respectively over software.

KRONOS Coprocessor · Keccak

A RISC-V Keccak co-processor shared across every NIST PQC standard.

Extends the ISA with three custom instructions over CV-X-IF, reused across CRYSTALS-Kyber, CRYSTALS-Dilithium, SPHINCS+ and FALCON.

On a Xilinx Artix-7 FPGA: up to 75% fewer clock cycles for 19% resource overhead.

LOKI Accelerator · Kyber

Low-latency, open-source Kyber accelerator IPs for a RISC-V microcontroller.

Speeds up the polynomial multiplications and hash functions at the core of CRYSTALS-Kyber key generation, encryption and decryption, integrated as an accelerator into a RISC-V microcontroller.

KALIPSO Accelerator · NTT/INTT

A memory-mapped NTT/INTT accelerator for CRYSTALS-Kyber, tuned for low DMA overhead.

Targets the transfer overhead between the RISC-V core and the accelerator IP using DMA. On an X-HEEP core it reaches up to 15.7× and 19.6× cycle-count improvement for NTT and INTT respectively over software.

keccak / keccak_integration Keccak · SHA-3

Reference Keccak/SHA-3 implementations and their integration into RISC-V SoCs.

Software, hardware and hybrid Keccak/SHA-3 implementations compared across area, frequency, clock cycles and efficiency, and integrated as an accelerator into the PULPissimo RISC-V microcontroller for CRYSTALS-Kyber — with speedups up to 2.79× over a plain software baseline at a 12.4% resource cost.

cva6 Work in progress · CV-X-IF

Integrating the team's cryptographic accelerators into the CVA6 application-class core.

CVA6 is a 6-stage, Linux-capable RISC-V core. The team is extending it with cryptographic accelerators over CV-X-IF and evaluating them for power side-channel leakage — RTL-level simulated power traces and FPGA measurements are compared using Correlation/Differential Power Analysis to check whether tightly integrated instruction pipelines leak information the ISE itself doesn't.
