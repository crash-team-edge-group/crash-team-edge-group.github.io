What the team works on

CRASH builds hardware for post-quantum and lightweight cryptography on RISC-V, from instruction-set extensions down to silicon.

  • Accelerators for post-quantum schemes — CRYSTALS-Kyber, CRYSTALS-Dilithium, Ascon, CROSS, HQC, LESS
  • RISC-V instruction-set extensions and coprocessors over the Core-V eXtension Interface (CV-X-IF)
  • Power side-channel analysis and countermeasures for cryptographic hardware
  • True random number generation and Keccak/SHA-3 hashing primitives
  • FPGA prototyping through to ASIC implementation
